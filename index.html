About us

One name only.

RetaEdge is your new one stop shop for all things retatrutide.

We make one thing only, so it is of the highest quality and purity.

The work sits in a small clean room, and retatrutide is the only peptide in it. Nothing is capped until the material has cleared a purity check, and that check is done in the United States.

Retatrutide starts from pharmaceutical-grade amino acids and is made to a set process. Each lot has to show two things: that the sample is clean, and that the molecule is retatrutide. The fill is checked as well, so the vial matches the milligrams on its label.

If a lot cannot show both, it does not wear the name. What does is a short list. 12 mg, 24 mg, 30 mg, and 40 mg. The lot mark on the glass stays with the record for that run.

A bigger order of retatrutide does not get an easier path. The same record follows the vial out of the room. These are research vials. They are not for human or animal use, and they are not FDA-approved.
